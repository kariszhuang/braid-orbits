## Exact lane

Four Rust threads enumerate all canonical index-additive first-pair frames in
degree10 and all10! third entries. Product one forces the fourth entry. All
transitive generated groups and passports are eligible. The scope is
identity-free, positive-genus everywhere-smooth components modulo simultaneous
S10 conjugation; genus0 and identity strata are intentionally omitted. A
complementary-pair rotation puts any everywhere-smooth component into a
first-pair-geodesic state. See the existing small-census MATH.md reduction.

POSITIVE_ONLY marks the changed population. Per-frame OMITTED.tsv counts
identity exclusions first and nonpositive-genus exclusions second. Enumerated
still counts every third rank; other coverage columns are scope restricted.
All eligible transitive starts receive literal negative braid words or exact
closed membership, or remain explicitly unresolved. A saved cap is not an
exclusion. Eight-byte ranked outcomes, immutable sources/binaries and COMPLETE
markers retain all evidence. Four independent Python audit processes later
reenumerate every rank, check omissions, pair masses, witnesses and six moves.
Producer completion alone is not census verification. No automatic mid-frame
resume is implemented; retain partial files and frozen sources on interruption.

Independent frame construction now follows all cycle-splitting transposition
paths from each product representative to identity. Every intermediate a is
geodesic, since index decreases by one per edge. Conversely any geodesic
p=a*b supplies such a path through a by peeling a shortest transposition
factorization of b. Thus this avoids a redundant n!-factor setup without
dropping a pair. Literal all-permutation equality is tested for every product
partition in degrees2--6. Third-entry audit still reenumerates every third rank.

## Guided lanes

Two single-CPU exploratory workers run alongside production and audit. One
cycles degrees11--14 with forest first pairs and prescribed-index third
entries. Another cycles degree12/14 block forests, with multiple moving
block-quotient entries and independent local voltages. These are different
from the exhausted one-local-entry/three-transversal-entry normal form.
Degree10 is reserved for exact coverage, avoiding duplicate discovery work.

Every transposition joins two separate components of a forest. Any ordering
of forest edges has index equal to its number of edges, so both entry indices
add to the product index. Block matchings are forests on points, not only on
blocks. Product one, positive genus and transitivity are checked literally.
With sorted indices a<=b<=c<=e, an everywhere-smooth passport must satisfy
a+e<=d-1 or b+c<=d-1: braid permutations can put the extreme pairing across
the two pairs, and a smooth pair's index sum cannot exceed d-1. Only this
necessary theorem filter and literal nonsmooth words reject starts.

Seeds are durable before bounded closure. Caps/timeouts are retained, not
false exclusions. Closed components and negative resolution words receive an
independent Python replay before VERIFIED; component deduplication across
batches and previous families is a separate interpretation step. Discovery
counts are occurrences, not unique components or exhaustive coverage. New
closed smoothness does not determine isotriviality; geometry stays unclassified
until independently established. Human review of mathematical proofs is pending.
