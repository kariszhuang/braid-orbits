# Second optimization pass

Question: increase useful identity-free positive-genus smooth discovery per
wall-clock second without changing exact acceptance, losing saved leads, or
weakening independent rejection verification. Current six-worker frozen search
continues during development; no competing discovery pool.

Scope: identical degree11--14 forest/block candidate streams, unchanged S_d
quotient and literal six-move closure. Bounded controls may use degrees4--25.
New measurements are justified because C149 excludes independent audit time.

Ranked falsifiable hypotheses:
1. Python per-lead parsing/statistics/replay is the pipeline bottleneck. A native
   independent streaming verifier will check identical records substantially
   faster; whole-job gain is less than verification-only gain.
2. Positive genus fixes a unique eligible complementary index sum. Early cycle
   counting against that target will speed rejection without changing witnesses.
3. Memory/file churn after amplification costs throughput. Stream both lead and
   resolution files, retain only exceptional positive/partial rows, and bound RAM.

Validation: same-record Python/native differential checks, malformed/truncated
and false-witness rejection, all66/25,192-state positive controls, Rust literal
controls, paired alternating-order timings including audit, and independent
small smoke jobs. Record source/binary hashes and bootstrap timing intervals.

Deployment: only after checks pass, request STOP on current root, wait for its
bounded jobs/audits and lock release, freeze a successor and inherit unresolved
verified and interrupted leads. Six slots total; no GPU (C149 hybrid slower),
no duration cap. Preserve predecessors and raw evidence on T7. A failure/cap is
never a negative certificate; successful closure still needs general audit.
